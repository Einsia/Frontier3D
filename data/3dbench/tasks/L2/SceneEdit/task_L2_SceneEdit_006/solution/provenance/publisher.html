Frosty Cyberpunk Interior Asset Pack

A downloadable asset pack

The original version of this asset pack was the result of 3 weeks work during the CYBRPUNK CREATIVE JAM 008, and recently extended a bit, while working on this new demo environment. Released under CC0.

All assets are made entirely from scratch * (except a few single pipe elements and the cable generator were made some time before and reused / edited though never released). The modular pipe system and the cable assets itself were completely made during the jam though. 

* The extended version includes a few more assets, all CC0, some which I made years ago:

from me

https://opengameart.org/content/colorful-bushes, based on
https://opengameart.org/content/cool-leaves-textures

https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-interface-textures

and the flower from
https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html


These models aren't textured or unwrapped, as they are meant to be used in other game jams or as a base to process and extend further (with a few exceptions)


Included are 618 exported meshes:
21 cable meshes + cable generator
317 pipe elements (including single elements and combined pipe systems)
28 ice / frozen parts
156 floor / ceiling and wall parts
and 96 other parts (props or parts of a prop)
+ demo scenes
