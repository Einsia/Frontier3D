## Measured edit protocol
The evaluator checks visible target silhouette/depth against the reference and separately checks preservation. Target names and mesh splits are unrestricted; retain actual editable 3D geometry.
Use both native views from the same saved scene. Appearance, shape and lighting tolerances remain published definitions with pending calibration.

## Structural geometry protocol
Geometry targets must have real nonzero thickness. Up to 48 visible target
locations with an observable reference back surface are probed along their
inward surface normal; at least four locations are needed and at least 90%
must have an oppositely facing back surface. Numerical ray epsilon is the larger
of 0.0000001 m and 0.0000001 times the reference target diagonal. The private
reference thickness is not required. Names and mesh splits are unrestricted.
These finite probes do not certify complete shells, watertightness or support.

## Required visible regions
The required regions below are checked independently. Each must cover at least
80% of its visible reference samples with actual added surface geometry at the
corresponding pixel. Depth tolerance is 3% of that region's visible 3D diagonal
(minimum 0.0000001 m); at least eight observable reference samples are needed.
Reference regions select visible geometry only: your object names and mesh splits
are unrestricted. An unobservable required region is reported as not measurable.
The global target silhouette still checks excess geometry. A large correct region
cannot compensate for a missing smaller region. These published thresholds with calibration limits
require calibration and do not certify hidden part counts, contacts or semantics.

## Cylindrical-container count
The three containers are checked on complete added geometry, including surfaces
occluded by the cloth. Object names, object counts, mesh splits and labels are
not answers. Hidden container shape is not required to match a private model.
The two visible groups (containers together, and cloth) are checked separately
under the required-region protocol above.

The geometry detector proposes up to 16 axes from area-weighted surface normals and
their cross products, and intersects the complete added surface with 33 planes
per axis. A section needs a closed circular polygon with at least six genuine
corners, a maximum angular gap of 60 degrees, RMS radial error <= 2.5% and maximum
radial error <= 6%. Collinear triangulation vertices are removed. Only axial
side surfaces with absolute normal/axis dot product <= 0.25 contribute.
At least four sections must persist; gaps of at most one plane are allowed.
Centres may differ by <= 10% of radius; section radii must stay within a factor
of 1.25. The measured span must be at least one radius and 40% of the visible
target's Z extent, and at most eight radii. Radii must be 4%-40% of the visible
target's 3D diagonal. Centres must be within the visible target bounding box
expanded by 25% of that diagonal. These broad scale/location limits use only
visible reference extent, not hidden barrel dimensions or positions.
Plane offsets use fractions (i+0.417)/33 of each projected extent. Welding
tolerance is max(0.0000001 m, visible diagonal * 0.000001). Repeated surfaces,
inner/outer shells and nearby axis proposals do not add to the count. Exactly
three persistent cylindrical structures are required. A disk, isolated ring,
box, sphere, existing scene object or render-hidden object cannot supply one.

These finite published probes need calibration. They do not prove continuous
shells, support/contact, all rim/band/cap details, or cloth non-intersection.

## Native edit measurements

Public numerical requirements are listed separately from private reference similarity.
The alternate camera translation is 0.30 m along local positive X (0.003 m tolerance).
Implemented exact counts require the stated integer; visual region coverage alone
does not prove a count. Unimplemented counts are explicitly reported as pending,
not satisfied, and are excluded from the implemented numerical mean. The result
remains provisional until numerical coverage and perceptual calibration are complete.

Geometry similarity compares the missing target's native visible surface, silhouette,
required regions and implemented thickness/cavity probes. Object names and mesh
splits are not answers; hidden geometry need not copy the private reference exactly.
The native geometry target term does not measure material appearance; the visual terms cover appearance. Do not change the supplied main camera.

Lighting similarity directly compares saved light energy and linear RGB color with
the reference scene; world background strength is included only where permitted.
Position, direction, type, dimensions, exposure, node links and other forbidden edits
affect preservation. Compare energy only within the same light type. Zero energy,
missing lights and wrong types cannot receive full target credit. Different light
parameters giving similar rendered pixels can still receive different scores.
No reference image metric is computed for lighting. Retain original lights and names.

## Scoring

Final item score: `20% numeric + 20% preservation + 20% vlm + 30% target + 10% detail`. Component definitions and evidence policies are in [Scoring](../../../../../../docs/en/scoring.md).

Missing content, shape errors and layout differences reduce the relevant quality terms. Technical failures or missing required evidence leave the score unresolved; missing weights are never redistributed or filled with zero. Delivery, execution, loading, dependencies and integrity are checked separately under the task contract. Bound saved-scene rerenders provide scoring evidence; submitted images and mismatch diagnostics are retained.

Numerical checks cover implemented, evidenced measurements only. Unimplemented exact counts are not treated as satisfied. Visual observations do not certify hidden geometry, physical contacts or complete physical correctness. Reference regions and perceptual judgments retain calibration limits.
