## Numerical geometry protocol (l2-brief-v2.1.0)
Use solid colors and procedural shading only. Image textures, environment images,
generated/baked image maps and image-dependent geometry/compositing are prohibited,
including packed or unused image nodes in used node trees. No private reference is used for scoring.
Assign bench_role to actual mesh geometry or to a parent grouping the mesh parts
of one entity. Object names are unrestricted. Roles select measurements and do not
prove semantics; visual fidelity and local detail are evaluated separately.
Street endpoints are derived from actual geometry as defined below;
old endpoint marker patches are not required. Counts group entity parts and reject identical
co-located copies. Dimensions use complete geometry and world metres; horizontal
sizes use a minimum-area rotated footprint. Camera heights use actual local ground.
Opening checks use interior surfaces and bounded through-depth rays. Route checks
use a 2.5D grid including vertical/thin surfaces, local floor height and low obstacles;
the ground band defaults to 0.1 m, or the measured vehicle height where specified.
This is not a general certification of human headroom or arbitrary stacked floors.
Grid uncertainty or capacity limits produce not_measurable, never an inferred pass.
Numerical tolerances, route step/slope settings and contact bands are published
definitions with pending calibration. The JSON below specifies this version exactly.

Roles: chimney, house, planter, property_entry, road, sidewalk_left, sidewalk_right, site, tree.

## Layout and course geometry (l2-brief-v2.3.0)
Sides and rows are measured relative to the actual straight path/road geometry,
not global X/Y or object names. All selected entity geometry must remain outside
the road/path's transverse interval. A row is a longitudinal center band spanning
at most 0.25 m; no equal row spacing or equal number per row is required.
For mini-golf, each lane must geometrically contain exactly one ball, cup and
diamond obstacle; lanes must be parallel within 2 degrees. Ball and cup occupy
opposite 15-percent end regions, and the diamond is at least 60 percent of the
way from ball to cup. These percentages and tolerances are published definitions
interpretations of start/far-end, not original hidden dimensions.
Each ball-to-cup path uses only that lane and its actual cup geometry. Start is
the ball's actual ground position and the goals are sampled actual cup-rim points.
Cup-to-lane connection is checked along 32 radial bands with 33 samples each.
Route clearance uses a conservative upright cylinder containing a 0.1 m ball,
not rolling dynamics. The 0.02 m grid has a reported width uncertainty; ambiguous
clearance produces not_measurable. These finite tests do not certify arbitrary
stacked surfaces, curved swept volumes, complete shells or text semantics.
All thresholds require calibration on accepted examples before formal release.

```json
[
  {
    "id": "house_side_layout",
    "metric": "l2_side_layout",
    "group": "numeric",
    "params": {
      "role": "house",
      "axis_role": "road",
      "per_side": 3,
      "rows_per_side": 0,
      "row_tolerance_m": 0.25
    }
  }
]
```


## Geometric assemblies (l2-brief-v2.4.0)
Whole-entity roles remain public geometry selectors, not semantic answers.
No private example shapes, dimensions or images are used. All thresholds below
are finite published definitions requiring accepted boundary examples and calibration.

For houses, additionally select chimney geometry with bench_role="chimney".
The evaluator derives attachment to a house from geometry, not parenting or names.
At least two distinct houses must have attached chimney geometry. Each chimney
needs a unique matching house. At least 80% of its valid footprint sections must
touch or intersect that house's top surface within 0.02 m, extend at least 0.1 m
above it, and have at most half the chimney's height embedded below it.
Sections use a 5x5 grid over the middle 80% of each oriented footprint; at least
half the positions must have actual upper/lower intersections separated by more
than 0.00001 m. Multiple chimneys on the same house do not add distinct houses.

For graves, select each whole grave as one bench_role="grave" entity. Bed and
marker may be separate meshes or joined; bench_part names are not answers.
The evaluator searches horizontal surface levels (Z variation <= 0.00001 m,
at most 128 levels) for a broad lower bed and a nearby upright upper volume.
Bed height must be positive and no more than half its short horizontal span.
At the candidate top level, actual horizontal area must cover at least 25% of
its oriented bounding rectangle. At least half the 5x5 footprint positions must
show nonzero bed thickness. The upper geometry must be non-coplanar, rise at
least 0.1 m above that level, and its height must be at least half its longest
horizontal span. It must start within 0.02 m of the bed-top level and remain
within the bed footprint expanded by 25% of the bed's longest horizontal span.
This permits a marker beside a bed, including a common joined mesh. Missing
parts, relabelled copies and lying plates cannot establish the required geometry.

These checks do not classify chimney/marker semantics, rounded/cross symbolism,
facade style, continuous arbitrary curved beds, complete connectivity, ground
support, entrances or perimeter closure. Those are separate engineering scopes;
textual semantics and ViT remain excluded from scores.

```json
  }
]
```


## Actual street and property access (l2-brief-v2.6.0)
The prior road/sidewalk endpoint-marker route checks are replaced. road_start,
road_end and sidewalk_*_start/end tags no longer define the required route.
Actual road geometry determines its longitudinal axis and ends. The two sidewalk
selection roles may be swapped: physical sides determine correspondence.
Sidewalk geometry must stay outside the actual straight-road projection. Each
physical side must span the real road ends and retain at least 1.2 m clear width;
the road retains its specified 6 m width. Complete finite cross-sections include
the ends, while disk-clearance connectivity uses width/2 + 3 grid cells of inset.
An additional height-continuous floor connection must reach the real outer ends;
inset clearance cannot hide a detached or raised last section. End-section widths
use a conservative two-cell error band and never round uncertain widths upward.
Every rasterized road and sidewalk cell is checked for obstruction, including
edge cells; a clear centerline alone is insufficient.

Bind actual driveway/entry surface geometry to bench_role="property_entry".
For each house, rays toward the house from the road at 0.05 m above its lowest
geometry locate the real front surface, at longitudinal intervals no larger than
0.1 m (maximum 256 probes). A selected entry surface must reach that surface
within 0.02 m horizontally and 0.02 m vertically, then connect to the actual
same-side sidewalk. The contact band uses 33 samples, and the entry route requires
0.8 m clear width as a published usability threshold with calibration limits. Shared paths and
merged entry meshes are allowed. Ground, endpoint markers, names, parents or
candidate-authored internal role strings cannot substitute for selected paths.

All three checks use complete triangles and a 0.02 m height-field grid, 1.8 m
standing clearance, 0.02 m steps, 0.5 maximum rise/run and 0.001 m coincidence
tolerance. Numerical width tolerance is 1%; the raster uncertainty is reported
and ambiguous clearance is not measurable. These finite measurements do not
certify doors, interior access, house style, structural support, stacked routes,
sub-grid defects or continuous 3D volume. Published geometric interpretations and
thresholds need accepted real boundary examples and human calibration.

## Scoring

Final item score: `60% vlm + 30% data + 10% detail`. Component definitions and evidence policies are in [Scoring](../../../../../../docs/en/scoring.md).

Missing content, shape errors and layout differences reduce the relevant quality terms. Technical failures or missing required evidence leave the score unresolved; missing weights are never redistributed or filled with zero. Delivery, execution, loading, dependencies and integrity are checked separately under the task contract. Bound saved-scene rerenders provide scoring evidence; submitted images and mismatch diagnostics are retained.

Numerical checks cover implemented, evidenced measurements only. Unimplemented exact counts are not treated as satisfied. Visual observations do not certify hidden geometry, physical contacts or complete physical correctness. Reference regions and perceptual judgments retain calibration limits.
