Blender Version: 2.66a, r55057
Model Version: 1.1

3D preview available @ https://sketchfab.com/show/wr3vp1zmLwuirmHT1umFmviAn6T

What is included:
1 x BA-2 base mesh (3380 vertices, 6964 Edges, 3616 faces, 6696 Tris, UV unwrapped)
1 x Diffuse map (2048 x 2048)
1 x Normal map (2048 x 2048)
1 x Bump map (2048 x 2048)
1 x Specular map (2048 x 2048)
1 x Emission map (2048 x 2048)
1 x Animation armature

Complete model is not manifold, but rather a composition of manifold "parts".  Each part is manifold and all parts were combined into a single mesh named "Bot.Mesh".

Maps included are all large 2048 x 2048.  Reason is they can be reduced but not grown, resize them to fit as needed.

Texture samples sourced from:
http://www.plaintextures.com/
http://www.goodtextures.com/
Textures were otherwise hand painted.

There is admittingly an error in the bump/normal mapping in the emission areas.  They are not very noticable, and with the emission map enabled they are concealed.

Hi everyone,

I am the chap running the goodtextures.com website.

I received an email earlier today from someone asking me to clarify the use of the textures. Hence why I am here.

Although the goodtexturs content is not currently licensed under CCO, the terms of use are very open for their usage.

So long as you use any of the textures in combination with something, be that a 3d model, website, game, background or anything really. You are absolutely free to do what you want with that piece of work. You can sell it, give it away, feed it to your dog or do anything you want. You will receive no objections from me.

My only objections would be if you were to take the textures as is, and then either sell them, upload them to a texture website or distribute them as a texture bundle for example.

On another note. I have been thinking for a long time now about making the whole site more open and maybe integrating the different CCO licenses similar to Deviantart. Just not sure how to do it at the moment.

I hope that answered some of your questions. Should anyone have any more queries, I would be more than happy to stay in touch.

Best regards,
