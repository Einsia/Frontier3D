Build an original, playable third-person 3D single-arena PvE roguelike in a restrained geometric style. The player starts with a pistol, survives escalating waves of enemies in one reusable arena, chooses upgrades between waves, and continues until victory or death. The game must be a real interactive loop rather than a fixed combat animation or a menu mock-up.

Create one enclosed combat arena approximately 32 m by 24 m with a readable floor, boundary walls or equivalent blockers, a few low obstacles and a clear spawn area. Keep the same arena across the run; reset actors and pickups between runs. Use a third-person camera that keeps the player, nearby enemies and aim direction readable without clipping through the arena. The art can be abstract and geometric, but characters, weapons, projectiles and enemies must have visible silhouettes and actual collision.

The player begins every new run with a pistol, 100 health, a 12-round magazine and no upgrades. Use documented controls: WASD movement, mouse aim, left mouse fire, R reload, Shift dash or evade if implemented, and Esc pause. The pistol must have a finite magazine, reload time and projectile or hitscan behavior that can be inspected in the project. Damage, fire interval, range and reload values must be declared in the README and must be applied by the running game. Do not give the player hidden damage, health or ammunition bonuses.

A stage consists of a wave followed by an upgrade choice. Start with six enemies in stage 1 and increase the total threat on later stages. Provide at least three enemy archetypes: a melee chaser that navigates toward the player and attacks at close range, a ranged attacker that keeps distance and fires visible projectiles, and a slower heavy enemy with higher health and a readable attack wind-up. Enemies must use real collision and navigation or steering, cannot teleport into attack range, and must not read future player inputs. Their health, movement, attack timing and projectile rules must be visible in the project or README.

After all enemies in a wave are defeated, pause combat and present three upgrade choices. The choices must be deterministic for a seed and must include at least these upgrade families across a run: weapon damage or projectile behavior, fire rate or magazine/reload, maximum health or healing, movement/dash equipment, and a defensive or utility effect. Applying a choice must update the running player state and HUD; the choice must persist into later stages and reset when a new run starts. Do not offer three labels that leave the underlying stats unchanged. The player may choose one card, then the next wave starts after a short transition.

Provide six stages or another clearly documented finite progression with increasing enemy count or strength. Stage 1 should be beatable with the starting pistol. Later stages must add pressure through composition, health, attack rate or arena spawn patterns rather than an unavoidable scripted hit. After the final stage, show a victory screen with the run score and selected upgrades. On death, show a game-over screen with restart and return-to-menu actions. Repeated restarts must remove old enemies, projectiles, pickups, timers and upgrade state.

Use a fixed 60 Hz gameplay update independent of render frame rate. The default random seed is 20260922 and must be changeable in a small settings panel or documented launch option. The seed controls enemy compositions and upgrade choices; the same seed plus the same player input sequence must reproduce those choices. Display the current stage, wave state, health, magazine, reload state, selected upgrades and score. Add restrained hit, reload, damage, upgrade and victory feedback. Local single-player only is required; do not implement network play.

The game must allow the evaluator to exercise the full loop: start with the pistol, defeat a wave, choose a meaningful upgrade, survive a stronger wave, die or complete the final stage, pause and restart. Submit the complete editable UE5 project and Blender source assets using the fixed delivery contract below.

The submitted project must use output/ue5_project/3DBenchGame.uproject as its fixed UE5 project entry. The project root must contain Config/ and Content/; Source/, Plugins/, and SourceAssets/Blender/ are allowed when used. Put a short launch and controls note in output/README.md. Do not submit generated Binaries/, Intermediate/, Saved/, or DerivedDataCache/ directories.
