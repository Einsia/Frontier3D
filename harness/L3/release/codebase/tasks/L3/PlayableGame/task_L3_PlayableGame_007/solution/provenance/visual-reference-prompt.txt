ONE landscape 1536x1024 visual direction concept image for an original small playable 3D game. Extremely simple art: bevelled cubes, spheres, cylinders, wedges, flat matte solid-color materials, soft studio lighting and contact shadows. No texture maps needed, no photorealism, no detailed characters, no realistic scenery, no logos, no writing, no numerals, no watermark, no collage. One coherent playable scene in a readable gameplay camera. Small abstract HUD icons only. The entire game should be feasible with basic Blender geometry and UE5. An elevated three-quarter view of a desktop marble track sandbox. A single white marble rolls down one continuously connected teal U-channel track made of visibly modular straight channels, two quarter-turn bends and two descending ramps, ending in an orange catch cup. Cubic posts physically support the high track. No disconnected floating tracks, no roller coaster loops. A few spare straight/bend/ramp pieces neatly beside the platform and a small bottom toolbar of simple shape icons suggest an editable sandbox. Every connection has a believable smooth ball path, no text.
