Build a small 3D miniature golf game. Use an elevated rotatable camera, a single ball, solid low walls, ramps and a recessed cup. Aim with the mouse, hold and release the left button to choose shot power, and display direction and power. A shot is allowed only while the ball is settled; once struck, movement follows gravity and contacts rather than a scripted path. Bank shots and slopes should matter. Use one consistent ball size and tune friction for controllable play.

Each hole has a tee, one cup and at least one obstacle or bend. Generate a connected, traversable course from a seed, using a construction method that guarantees a ball-width route; never place the cup behind an impassable wall. The first hole teaches a straight shot, then mix turns, banks and ramps. Show the next hole number and let the player continue for arbitrarily many holes, or replay the current hole. Include a practice option to select a seed.

Count one stroke per accepted shot. Going out of bounds returns the ball to its last valid shot position and adds exactly one penalty stroke. A cup completes only when the ball enters and settles in its physical capture region; passing above it must not count. Freeze the completed-hole score, then offer the next hole. Display current strokes and cumulative strokes; avoid an unverified par system. Restarting a hole clears that hole's strokes; a new run clears the run total.

Use Blender + UE5 to deliver an editable, actually playable 3D game. Keep the art deliberately simple: basic solids, small solid-color palettes, simple lights and readable silhouettes; external texture packs, detailed humans and elaborate environments are not required. Provide a short in-game controls guide, visible feedback for important actions, pause/resume and restart without accumulated actors or timers. Gameplay timing must use simulation time rather than render-frame counts. Seeded content/choices should reproduce on the same version; bitwise-identical physics across machines is not required. For randomized content use default seed 20260922 and let the player change it.
