Build a three-dimensional spaceflight sandbox for repeated design, launch and orbit inspection. Let players freely combine variants of fuel tanks, engines, command pods, stack separators, radial decouplers and parachutes. Give parts sensible data, including dry mass, fuel capacity, thrust, specific impulse and parachute operating conditions. Allow multiple instances of the same part model. Provide an editable staging list so players can schedule ignition, stack or radial separation and parachute deployment, as well as control throttle and attitude. Engines must stop producing thrust when fuel is exhausted. Separation must correctly update mass, connections and thrust sources; parachutes cannot generate aerodynamic drag in vacuum. Include at least the Sun, Earth and Moon. Real terrain and astronomical scale are not required, but units and physical scales must be internally consistent. At minimum, simulate gravity around one current central body and provide an orbit view and key orbital parameters; multi-body gravity and a Moon landing are not required. Use single-body gravity during unpowered vacuum flight, and account for thrust, fuel use and changing mass during powered flight. Support at least 3 distinct valid vehicle designs and return to design or restore saved state after a failed flight. The concept image is only a visual reference; its composition, trajectory and proportions are not targets to reproduce. Use physics fixed at 20 Hz; time acceleration advances multiple base steps. The default seed is 20260914. Use m and m/s for position and velocity, kg for mass, s for time, N for thrust and s for specific impulse. Saving and loading must restore vehicles, detached bodies, connections, fuel, attitude, staging and orbital state.
